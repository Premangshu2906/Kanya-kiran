Who we are?

Kanya Kiran is a flagship initiative of Kalinga Institute of Industrial Technology (KIIT) and Kalinga Institute of Social Sciences (KISS) committed to building a safer, more inclusive society where every girl can learn, grow, and thrive without fear. Our work focuses on four key pillars: physical safety, education, cyber safety, and psychological & emotional well-being.

Kanya Kiran is a platform for everyone, because creating a safer future for girls is a shared responsibility. We engage girls, boys, men, women, families, communities, and institutions to foster awareness, encourage meaningful conversations, and drive lasting change.

Our Mission

To raise awareness about physical violence, cyber violence, and psychological and emotional violence, and their impact on girls and young people.

To promote accessible and inclusive conversations around safety, consent, respect, dignity, and equality across communities.

To champion education as a foundation for empowerment, ensuring every girl has the opportunity to learn, grow, and fulfil her potential.

To promote safer physical and digital spaces where girls can live, learn, express themselves, and participate without fear.

To engage girls, boys, men, women, families, communities, and institutions in creating a culture of awareness, accountability, and collective responsibility.

To foster an environment of ZERO TOLERANCE towards violence, discrimination, harassment, and practices that undermine the safety, dignity, and rights of girls.
